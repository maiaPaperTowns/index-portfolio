Hobbies and Interests

Creative Stuff

  • Photography and Video - I shoot a lot of campus events and just random moments I find interesting. I spend way too long color grading things in Premiere and DaVinci Resolve.
  • Digital Art - I draw in Procreate mostly, sometimes Illustrator. A lot of what I make ends up as posters or promotional graphics for theatre shows.
  • Watching Films - Having a film minor means I have an excuse to watch a lot of movies. I'm really into documentaries and international cinema.

Tech and Making Things

  • Personal Web Projects - I like building random things just to see if I can. Not everything has a practical purpose.
  • 3D Modeling and Printing - I design in Blender and print with Cura. I've made props for a couple of theatre productions this way.

Other Things I'm Into

  • Theatre - I do the marketing and publicity for DePauw Theatre shows, but I also just really like going to see live performances.
  • Music - I mess around in Logic Pro and edit audio for the D3 Network show. Nothing serious, just fun.
